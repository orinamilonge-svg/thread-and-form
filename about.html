About

An approach to modern dressing.

Thread & Form is a contemporary womenswear concept built around thoughtful silhouettes, versatility and a personal approach to style.

Our visual and design direction explores pieces that feel refined without being restrictive, bringing together modern expression and the ease of everyday dressing.

We believe clothing should complement the person wearing it, not overwhelm them.

Thread & Form is a fictional fashion brand created as a website design portfolio project.
